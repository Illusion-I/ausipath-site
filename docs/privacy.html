Ausipath Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 October 2026

Ausipath is a study app for the Australian citizenship test. It is provided by Ausipath ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have.

If you have a question about this policy or your information, contact us at supportausipath@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You can use the free parts of Ausipath without an account. If you do, your study progress stays on your phone and we receive nothing about you.
  • If you create an account, we store your email address, your study progress and your subscription status, so that they follow you to your other devices.
  • We do not show advertising, we do not sell your information, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • You can delete your account, and everything saved with it, from inside the app at any time.

Information we collect

If you use Ausipath without logging in

Your study progress, bookmarks, settings, test date and reminders are stored only on your phone. None of it is sent to us.

If you create an account or log in

Information we do not collect

  • We never receive your card or payment details. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple or Google.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics services in the app.
  • Study reminders are created on your phone. Turning them on does not send us anything.

How we use your information

We use the information above only to:

  • create and secure your account and log you in;
  • save your study progress and bring it to your other devices;
  • check whether you have a current subscription and unlock paid content;
  • correct errors in the content when you report them;
  • respond when you contact us;
  • meet our legal obligations.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell it.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the companies that help us run Ausipath, and only as needed for them to do so.

Where your information is kept and how it is protected

  • Our database is hosted in Sydney, Australia.
  • Information travels between the app and our servers over encrypted connections.
  • Each account can read and change only its own data.
  • The login session on your phone is kept in the phone's encrypted storage (the iOS Keychain or the Android Keystore).

No system is perfectly secure. If we become aware of a data breach that is likely to cause you serious harm, we will tell you and the regulator as the law requires.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your login, your study progress and your subscription record from our servers, and ask our payment service to erase its record of you. Copies may remain in routine backups for a short time before they are overwritten. Apple and Google keep their own records of your purchases under their own policies.

Your choices and rights

  • See or change your information. Your email address and plan are shown under Account on the More tab. Contact us to ask for a copy of your information or to have it corrected.
  • Reset your progress. Settings → Reset all progress deletes your study progress from your phone and from your account.
  • Log out. Logging out removes your study progress from the phone. It stays in your account.
  • Delete your account. More → Account → Delete account permanently deletes your account and everything saved with it, and clears the app on your phone. You can also ask us to do this: see https://illusion-i.github.io/ausipath-site/delete-account.html.
  • Cancel a subscription. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local law, such as the right to object to or restrict certain uses of your information. Contact us to exercise them.

Children

Ausipath is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Complaints

If you have a concern about how we have handled your information, please contact us first at supportausipath@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the effective date above, and if a change is significant we will tell you in the app or by email.
