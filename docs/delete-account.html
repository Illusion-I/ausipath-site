Delete your Ausipath account

This page explains how to delete your Ausipath account and the information saved with it. Ausipath is provided by Ausipath.

Delete it in the app (immediate)

  1. Open Ausipath and log in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open the More tab.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Or ask us to delete it

If you cannot use the app, email supportausipath@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email. We may ask you to confirm that the address is yours before we act.

What is deleted

  • Your account and login, including any link to Google or Apple sign-in.
  • Your email address and account ID.
  • Your study progress saved to the account: answers, practice and mock test results, lesson progress, bookmarks and achievements.
  • Any reports you sent about a question.
  • Our record of your subscription.
  • The record of you held by our payment service, RevenueCat.

When you delete the account in the app, the app on that phone is also cleared: your progress, bookmarks, reminders and settings.

What is kept

  • Backups. Copies may remain in routine backups for a short time before they are overwritten.
  • Apple and Google. They keep their own records of your purchases under their own policies. We cannot delete those.
  • Anything the law requires us to keep, such as records needed for tax.

Cancel your subscription separately

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. If you have one, cancel it first, or you may be charged again:

  • iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Ausipath → Cancel Subscription.
  • Android: Google Play → your profile picture → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions → Ausipath → Cancel subscription.

Deleting only your progress

To keep your account but clear your study history, open the app and go to More → Settings → Reset all progress. This deletes your progress from the phone and from your account.
